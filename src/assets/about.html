%%THIS_SITE%%

And why you might want to use it

%%THIS_SITE%% is hosted at %%ORIGINAL_SITE_URL%%.

Better integration* of %%THIS_SITE%% with the original %%ORIGINAL_SITE%% requires Tampermonkey and this script: %%USERSCRIPT_URL%%


%%THIS_SITE%% isn’t a separate chat room from %%ORIGINAL_SITE%%. It mirrors all the comments that are posted there, and comments you post on %%THIS_SITE%% are posted back to the original chat room too. You’re in the same conversation here or there.

This chat facility exists to provide improved chat features, and, for those who care (and agree), a more pleasing look and feel.

What are some of the extra features? Starting with image and file sharing:

  • You can paste images from your clipboard directly into the message input field, generating links to those images which others can click on when you send your messages.
  • You can drag files (typically images or video) into the message input field, also creating links.
  • Further, you can click the upload button as well, which is often the easiest way to send an image or file from a mobile device.
Where do these files that you post go? By default they get sent to the %%ORIGINAL_SITE_UPLOADER%% page. And that’s still where you’d go if you decide you want to delete something after you send it. (Don’t forget to use a tripcode if you want to be able to delete files from the uploader page yourself.)
 
You also can optionally send images to %%EXTERNAL_UPLOAD_SITE%%. You might want to do this for off-topic content that %%ORIGINAL_SITE%% would rather not host, or for private sharing when using the %%THIS_SITE%%’s Direct Messaging feature. %%EXTERNAL_UPLOAD_SITE%% also provides the advantage of percentage-done feedback when uploading large files. One big warning, however: %%EXTERNAL_UPLOAD_SITE%% is convenient and useful, but it does not excel at reliability. Don't be too surprised if uploads fail, or later attempts to use the links to your uploads fail. %%EXTERNAL_UPLOAD_EXTRA_INFO%%

Other features?

  • As just mentioned, Direct Messaging. By default people can’t bother you by sending you DMs that you might not want. You have to check the “Allow DMs” option to receive DMs. You can only send or receive DMs from people using %%THIS_SITE%% who have also enabled DMs.
  • If you see someone in the list of Participants with a by their name, you can click on their name and start up a private chat. If someone else sends you a DM, a tab called “Main Chat” will appear (if it wasn’t present already) and a new tab with the sender’s name will appear, which you can either click on to enter the chat, or close without ever reading.
  • You can edit or delete your own messages by hovering over or tapping on them. It should be noted, however, that only %%THIS_SITE%% users will see these edits and deletions. Your typos and regretted words will live on at %%ORIGINAL_SITE_LONG%%.
  • Image preview thumbnails for image links in messages.
  • Selectable light and dark color themes. And there’s even a theme called “Like It’s 1999” that will restore much of the... um, let’s call it “nostalgic” look of the original %%ORIGINAL_SITE%%. If you’re into that sort of thing.
  • WYSIWYG text entry for doing things like italic and bold text, without needing to use BBCode.
  • Easy access to many emoji.
  • Better tracking of who’s in the chat room, and if they’re idle or not. This improved tracking works best for other users of %%THIS_SITE%%. When a %%THIS_SITE%% user has been inactive for ten minutes or more, their name will be dimmed in the Participants list.
  • Greater reliability maintaining a steady connection to the chat room, as the original design can be broken by a single failed attempt to read the latest messages, from which it will not recover until the user refreshes their browser.

More on Direct Messaging

For privacy, DMs are encrypted so they can’t easily be read from the database where they are briefly kept. They also have a life span of two hours, after which they are permanently deleted.

What do I mean by “better integration”?

If you use %%THIS_SITE%% simply by going to %%ORIGINAL_SITE_URL%% all of the extra features work fine, but your messages back at %%ORIGINAL_SITE_LONG%% will look a bit odd. Instead of seeing a message from you looking like, say, “ringo◆sko5SeEpMc > What you said.”, what will be seen is “CHAT②◆uwC.eL5B4M > 《ringo◆sko5SeEpMc》What you said.”.
 
If you use the Tampermonkey userscript, however, you’ll get to be your own person back at %%ORIGINAL_SITE_LONG%%, rather than just one of many being chaperoned in the conversation by CHAT②.
